# Fixauto

Fixauto coordinates vehicle repair on a customer's behalf through external specialists.

## Language

**Customer**: The person requesting and purchasing Fixauto's service; they may be an authorized representative rather than the vehicle owner.

**Vehicle owner**: The person whose authority over the vehicle must be evidenced for handover and service authorization.

**Service case**: One managed journey for one vehicle, from request through diagnosis, repair, return, and any related warranty complaint.
_Avoid_: Order, ticket, booking when referring to the entire journey.

**Coordinator**: The named Fixauto person accountable for a service case and customer communication.

**Diagnostic authorization**: Customer permission for specified inspection and its disclosed charges; it is distinct from repair approval.

**Repair estimate**: An itemized proposed scope, cost, and timeline submitted for customer approval.

**Repair approval**: A customer's authorization of a particular repair estimate version.

**Management fee**: Fixauto's disclosed charge for coordinating the service, separate from workshop and parts charges.

**Handover**: A recorded transfer of vehicle custody between people or organizations.

**Quality check**: Fixauto's documented assessment of completed work before return.

**Repair warranty**: The written, limited promise attached to approved repair work; distinct from the vehicle manufacturer's warranty.

**Warranty complaint**: A reported post-return problem assessed against the original case and its warranty terms.
